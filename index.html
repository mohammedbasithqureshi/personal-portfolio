About

I'm a full-stack developer and product builder based in Hyderabad. My work sits at the intersection of regional access and modern web engineering — taking the same rigor used to build enterprise SaaS and pointing it at problems that mainstream platforms treat as an afterthought.

Most of what I ship is aimed at first-generation job seekers and students across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh: recruitment exam prep, job discovery, and study-abroad guidance, built bilingually and designed to run on modest devices and patchy connections.

Stack of choice: Next.js, TypeScript, React, and Tailwind, shipped fast on Vercel with lean, dependency-light infrastructure.
